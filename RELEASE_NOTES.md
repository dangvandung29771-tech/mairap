## FRONTIER — Vanilla+ Expansion 1.0.0

A polished Vanilla+ expansion for **Minecraft 1.21.1** on **NeoForge 21.1.x**.

### Install
1. Install [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/) 21.1.x for Minecraft 1.21.1.
2. Drop **`frontier-1.0.0.jar`** into your `.minecraft/mods/` folder.
3. No hard dependencies; Curios is optional (a built-in 2-slot relic pouch is included).

### What's inside
- **Master Grindstone** — 3D workstation with animated wheel, glowing runes and sparks; BEFORE/AFTER stat comparison GUI, max 2 forged properties per weapon, controlled levels with diminishing returns.
- **Forging materials** — Sharp, Balanced and Heavy whetstones; Precision, Blood and Void crystals. Server-authoritative; forged weapons reflect their properties via subtle emissives and geometry, not particle spam.
- **Ancient Crypts** — 4 hand-built dungeons below the deepslate: Fallen Barracks, Forgotten Mine, Ritual Crypt and Royal Tomb, each with unique layouts, traps, puzzles, hidden areas and environmental storytelling.
- **New enemies** — Copper Shield Skeleton (blocks frontal attacks) and Gold Digger Zombie (digs and places blocks), both fully 3D models with keyframe animations (anticipation, impact, follow-through).
- **6 relics** — Climber's Claw, Feather of Grace, Infernal Ring, Explorer Compass, Stoneheart, Blood Pendant; limited to 2 active slots; dedicated Relic GUI; Curios-compatible when present.
- **Vehicles** — Large Raft (4 passengers, chest + furnace, bobbing oars) and Pocket Airship (coal-fueled, fuel-limited flight with a compact HUD; deliberately does not replace the Elytra).
- **Irrigation & soil** — Wood Canal, Stone Pipe, Sprinkler (radius 5), Enriched Farmland and Compost Cake; improves growth without full automation.
- **Progression** — Ancient Shards currency, dedicated Frontier creative tab, custom sound events, unified parchment-and-iron GUI style.
